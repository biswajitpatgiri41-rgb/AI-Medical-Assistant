# AI-Medical-Assistant
Nexus Health AI is a specialized clinical and administrative normalization engine designed to solve Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) inefficiencies. It acts as an intelligent layer between unstructured hospital data and structured payer requirements, ensuring that clinical documentation aligns perfectly with financial claims.

🏥 Nexus Health AI: Clinical & Administrative Normalization EngineNexus Health AI is an intelligent orchestration layer designed to bridge the gap between unstructured clinical documentation and structured healthcare administration (RCM). It solves the "Normalization Problem" in healthcare by transforming messy, handwritten, or digital medical records into interoperable FHIR R4 resources and audit-ready insurance claims.🚀 Key FeaturesMultimodal Data Ingestion:Unified pipeline for digital PDFs, Word documents, and scanned images.Advanced image preprocessing (grayscale, contrast enhancement, and noise reduction) to handle low-quality medical scans.AI-Powered OCR & Extraction:Utilizes NVIDIA NeMo Retriever-OCR v1 for high-precision text extraction from complex medical layouts.Leverages DeepSeek-R1-8B and GPT-OSS-120b models to parse clinical entities, medications, and financial line items.Administrative Data Normalization:Automatically maps unstructured "clinical narratives" to standardized medical codes (ICD-10 for diagnoses and CPT for procedures).Extracts critical metadata required for payers, including Provider NPIs and Tax IDs.Interoperable FHIR R4 Mapping:Converts parsed data into official FHIR resources: Patient, Condition, Claim, and ExplanationOfBenefit (EOB).Ensures cross-platform compatibility for EHR write-backs and TPA integrations.Revenue Reconciliation Logic (RCM):Revenue Leakage Detection: Identifies clinical procedures documented in notes but missing from the final bill.Medical Necessity Scrubbing: Validates that every billed line item is supported by a documented diagnosis.Intelligent Claim Scrubbing:Predicts the probability of insurance denial using an AI-driven risk scoring engine.Generates an administrative audit log to track normalization changes (e.g., mapping "headache" to ICD-10 G44.1).🛠️ Technical StackFrontend: Streamlit (Production-grade Dashboard)AI Infrastructure: NVIDIA NIM (NeMo Retriever, Llama-Vision, GPT-OSS-120b)Data Standards: HL7 FHIR R4, ICD-10, CPTOCR Engine: NeMo Retriever OCR v1Language: Python 3.10+Libraries: OpenAI SDK, Pandas, Plotly (Analytics), FPDF (Structured Reporting)📂 Repository Structure (For GitHub)FileResponsibilityapp.pyMain Stakeholder UI;
